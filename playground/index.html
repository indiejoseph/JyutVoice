Cantonese text-to-speech with English words mixed in · JyutVoice · your reference clip sets the voice

Any clip of the voice you want, at least 0.5 s; only the first 30 s are used. Only the speaker's voice print is taken from it, so no transcript is needed. A clean, close-up recording of 5–10 s works best.
